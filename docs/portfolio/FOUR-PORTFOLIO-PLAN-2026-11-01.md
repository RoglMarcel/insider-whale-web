Owner selected 15% activation / 10% trailing distance with next-available-close execution on 2026-11-01. Retain the 25% loss trigger, 90-calendar-day hold and no fixed target. This is an explicitly chosen experimental policy, not an empirically optimized strategy.

1. Apply the policy to the overlay and cash-based concentrated portfolio through current defaults and an idempotent configuration-v4 migration. Archive the previous settings and complete books before migration/replay. Preserve sizing, capital, inception and unrelated settings. Mark historical results retrospective under the new policy.
2. Add `alerts >80 only` as a fourth curve: exact score >80, same capital/calendar/costs/exits/20% target/five-slot/idle-cash rules as portfolio 3. Distinct observed high-score qualification determines entry time; never upgrade an earlier low-score entry with a later score. Keep all >=70 observations for research and preserve experiment candidates after rolling pruning.
3. Build, persist and publish all active books atomically with their configs, provenance, skipped entries, stale marks and pending exits. Include the fourth book in desktop/shared publication without duplicating the original main-book Backtest archive.
4. Show four summary cards and four chart series with aligned dates/capital and shared costs. Give the fourth book its own positions, rules, stats and quality details. Retain compatibility with older snapshots lacking a fourth book; show pending rather than substituting another portfolio. SPY remains the market benchmark. Strategy allocations share signals and are dependent comparisons.
5. Verify strict 80/80.1 boundaries, first-qualified timestamps (including after-close crossings), defaults/migration/restart/archival, identical 3-vs-4 controls, absence of qualifying signals, capacity/cash/cooldown, delayed exits and deterministic replay.
